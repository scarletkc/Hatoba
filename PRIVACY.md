# Privacy policy

Hatoba is an open-source SSH client, made by ScarletKc. It has no telemetry, no analytics, and no ads, and it sends nothing about you to its developer on its own.

This policy covers the Hatoba apps and the website at [hatoba.fog.moe](https://hatoba.fog.moe).

## On your device

Your data, such as hosts, keys, and passwords, is kept in an encrypted vault on your device. Some credentials are kept in the system's credential store. Hatoba also writes local logs for troubleshooting, which stay on your device unless you share them.

## What leaves your device

- **Connections you make.** Hatoba connects to the servers you choose.
- **Sync, if you turn it on.** Your data is encrypted on your device before it is uploaded to storage in your own account. The storage provider can see the encrypted data and its metadata, such as the number and size of items, times, and IP addresses, but not its contents.
- **The AI assistant, if you set it up.** Hatoba sends the AI providers you set up what the assistant needs to work, such as your messages and attachments, terminal output, host names, and the results of the tools it runs. Other services you set up for it, such as web search or MCP servers, receive what they need to do their part. Anything that appears in a terminal, a tool result, or text you write can reach these services.
- **Updates and public data.** Hatoba checks for new versions, downloads updates, and fetches public data such as model information. These requests carry nothing that identifies you.
- **Links you open.** Links that Hatoba opens in your browser can include basic details, such as the app version and your operating system.

Each of these services sees your IP address and handles what it receives under its own privacy policy.

## The website

hatoba.fog.moe is a static site. It sets no cookies and has no analytics or tracking. Your browser remembers the language you pick, and nothing is sent anywhere for it. The site's hosting providers see the requests your browser makes, as for any website.
